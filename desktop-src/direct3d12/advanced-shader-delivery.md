# Advanced Shader Delivery

Advanced Shader Delivery precompiles Direct3D 12 shaders and delivers them to players at download time. This reduces first-launch load times, in-game shader compilation stutter, and power consumption.

For documentation on how to adopt Advanced Shader Delivery for your title, see [Advanced Shader Delivery](https://aka.ms/xgd-asd-selfservice).
